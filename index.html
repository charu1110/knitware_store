Made in Ludhiana

The hosiery capital, and we grew up in it.

Panwar Knitwear was founded by Mohar Singh Panwar in Ludhiana, Punjab — the centre of India's knitwear trade. Three decades later the family still runs the floor: Prabhu Panwar and Bhala Ram Panwar as co-founders and CEOs.

  • Premium knits sourced and stocked before the season starts
  • Rigorous piece-level quality checks on every batch
  • Eco-conscious processes and low-waste cutting
  • Listed and rated on JustDial, IndiaMART and Google
