Before you order

The questions buyers always ask.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Most styles start at 50 pieces per design for hoodies and sweatshirts, and 100 pieces for T-shirts and bottom wear. Colour and size splits within that quantity are up to you.

How long does an order take?

Sampling takes about a week once the spec is agreed. Bulk usually ships 15–25 days after sample approval, depending on quantity, printing and the season — winter months run tighter.

Can you make garments under my own brand?

Yes. Private label is a large part of what we do — your woven label, printed neck tag, hang tag, poly bag and carton markings, all applied on our floor.

Do you send samples before bulk?

Always. You approve fit, shade and trims on a physical garment before a single roll of fabric is cut. Sample charges are adjusted against the bulk order.

Do you ship outside Punjab?

We despatch across India by road and courier, and handle export documentation for overseas buyers. Freight is quoted separately once the carton count is known.
