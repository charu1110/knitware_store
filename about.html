A family floor in the hosiery capital.

Panwar Knitwear has been cutting and stitching in Ludhiana, Punjab for close to three decades — long enough to know which fabrics survive a season and which only survive a photo shoot.

Who we are

Manufacturers, not middlemen.

We are a knitwear manufacturer based in Ludhiana, Punjab, India, specialising in high-quality top and bottom wear. Our range covers T-shirts, track pants, lowers, shorts, sweatshirts and hoodies, sold under two labels — ZONIXA for everyday wardrobe pieces and MSP Sports for active and team wear.

Because the whole process sits under one roof, a buyer talks to the same people who run the cutting table. That is the difference between a promise about lead time and an actual one.

  • Own manufacturing facility with current-generation machinery
  • Piece-level quality checks before packing
  • Eco-conscious, low-waste production practices
  • Verified presence on JustDial, IndiaMART and Google

Our craftsmanship

From yarn to poly bag.

Six stages, all in house. Nothing is subcontracted out of sight.

  • Stage 01

    Fabric selection

    We choose from Spun Fleece, Dry Fit, Honeycomb Lycra, 100% Cotton, Cotton Lycra, NS Bonded, Russian Fleece and Sherpa — matched to your GSM and price point.

  • Stage 02

    Sampling

    A physical sample before bulk. You approve fit, shade and trims on the actual garment, not a render.

  • Stage 03

    Cutting

    Layered cutting with marker planning that keeps wastage down and sizing consistent across the run.

  • Stage 04

    Stitching

    Dedicated lines per garment type, so hoodie operators stitch hoodies all season and get faster at it.

  • Stage 05

    Branding & finishing

    Screen and sublimation printing, embroidery, woven labels and hang tags applied on our own floor.

  • Stage 06

    Check & pack

    Every piece is inspected, measured against the size chart, pressed, poly-bagged and cartoned for dispatch.
